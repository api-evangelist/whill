# WHILL

> WHILL, Inc. designs and manufactures enhanced personal mobility solutions — electric power chairs and mobility scooters (Model C2, Model F, Modo Breeze ultralight wheelchair, and Model R three- and four-wheel scooters) — and operates an autonomous mobility service that has completed over 700,000 rides worldwide. This is a consumer hardware and mobility-service company; as of this writing it publishes no public developer API, SDK, or developer portal.
